Calorie-burn assumptions

Use your estimated sedentary maintenance calories (not a diet target). Baseline includes resting daily energy and ordinary light activity. Added exercise counts only the estimated burn above rest to avoid double counting.

A default of 2,200 kcal/day is only a placeholder. Your true maintenance depends on age, body composition and usual activity, and can vary substantially. Weight trends are more useful than a single day's estimate.

Important health note

The original 180 lb by December 20 goal may require an unsafe rate of loss depending on your starting weight. A common sustainable range for many adults is approximately 1–2 lb per week, though individual needs vary. This app tracks your chosen goal without prescribing crash dieting, starvation, or excessive exercise. Consult a clinician for a personalized target.

Protect your data

Your information stays in this browser on this device. It is not uploaded to a server. Export a backup regularly; clearing Safari website data may delete your entries.

When hosted with HTTPS, use Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Install and use the app consistently in the same Safari/Home Screen context; browser and standalone storage may differ.

Privacy & reset

No login, tracking scripts, advertisements, or third-party data collection are built into this app.
